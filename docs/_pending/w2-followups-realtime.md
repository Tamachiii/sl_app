## docs/INVARIANTS.md — Messaging / notifications

In the bullet "**A message event changes the entries that show its message, and only them; it never invalidates the `['messages']` root.**", replace the text from "an unread count moves only by what a **whole** entry" to "(`patchCached`)." with:

an unread count moves only by what a **whole** entry (cached, not invalidated) says the message was before, so one change applied twice — by the mutation and by its echo, by two bindings — counts once, and the id of a deleted message is remembered for that. The pair's thread and the conversation list are read at different times, and a change that lands between the two reads is in one of them only: what the later read says wins (`laterState` in `applyMessageUpsert`) — a list that holds the message as its last one over a thread that lacks it, a row that is read over one that is not (`read_at` is the one column the update trigger leaves to change, and the app sets it once and never clears it). A change that a read already had counts once when the thread or the list's last message holds it; a count read on its own between a change and its event, with neither of them holding it, counts the change a second time until the next resync — nothing in the cache tells that case apart. When no whole entry can say, the exact keys `['messages', 'unread-count', me]` and `['messages', 'conversations', me]` are read again, gathered for `REFRESH_DELAY_MS` so a burst of N events asks once (`createRefresher`); `payload.old` is never read for a column (it is the primary key alone). An entry marked as behind, or not read yet, is not patched (writing it would mark it current, or create it), and any entry with a read in flight when its event arrives is read again (`patchCached`, `refreshInFlight` in [lib/realtimeCache.js](../src/lib/realtimeCache.js)): at once when it is shown with data — the read is cancelled and sent again —, and otherwise once that read has answered. An invalidation only joins a first read, or the read of an entry nobody shows, and that read's answer — sent before the change — clears the mark, so `createRefresher` keeps such a key, a round of `REFRESH_DELAY_MS` at a time, until its read has answered. A DELETE, which carries the id alone, also reads again a thread with a read in flight that does not hold the id.

## docs/INVARIANTS.md — Messaging / notifications

Replace the whole bullet that starts "**The chat and bell channels outlive a lost connection, and the cache is read again whenever events may have been missed.**" with:

- **The chat and bell channels outlive a lost connection, and the cache is read again whenever events may have been missed — and not at every switch of tab.** Both hooks open their channel through `watchPostgresChanges` in [lib/realtimeChannel.js](../src/lib/realtimeChannel.js), the only code that calls `supabase.channel` or `supabase.removeChannel`. **A Realtime channel is never removed from an error status**: `CHANNEL_ERROR` and `TIMED_OUT` leave it to the client, which joins it again by itself, and a channel removed on its first error is never joined again — the chat and the bell then stay as they are until a reload, after every dropped socket and every iOS suspension. It is removed only after `MAX_JOIN_FAILURES` failed joins in a row with no `SUBSCRIBED` between them (a join the server refuses every time), and opened again with a new count on the next `online` or `visibilitychange`; a channel the server closed is opened again then, or once it is `REOPEN_AFTER_MS` old. Each opening has a topic of its own (`<topic>-<n>`): the client hands back a channel that is still leaving under the same name. `onResync(reason)` is called with one of `RESYNC`: `subscribed` on every `SUBSCRIBED`, the first included (what the page read while the channel was joining reached it on no channel), never held back; `online` on every `online`; `visible` on `visibilitychange` to visible only after an absence of `RESYNC_AFTER_HIDDEN_MS` (30 s, past one 25 s heartbeat of realtime-js) or more, after an absence that was not seen, or with the channel not joined — joining, in error, closed or let go. A desktop tab that is switched away from keeps its socket and is handed every event meanwhile, so a short absence with the channel joined reads nothing; a socket that dies during one is closed or fails its heartbeat, the client joins the channel again, and that `SUBSCRIBED` reads everything, a heartbeat or two later at worst. `online` and `visible` together ask once (`RESYNC_GAP_MS`), and a visible that reads nothing leaves that gap alone. Each hook answers with an invalidation of its root whose options are `resyncRefetchOptions(reason)`: `{ cancelRefetch: false }` after `online` and `visible`, so a read in flight is joined — it is most often React Query's own refetch on reconnect or focus, and a cancel would only send a second request (the thread, the conversation list and the bell's list do not pass `signal` on, so the first is not even stopped); `{ cancelRefetch: true }` after `SUBSCRIBED`, and for a resync that gives no reason, since a read sent before the join may be older than the first event the channel delivers. A read that stalled while the page was away is joined too; if the socket died meanwhile, the `SUBSCRIBED` of the channel joined again sends it again. The messages resync leaves `['messages', 'session-refs', …]` alone: it reads `sessions`.
