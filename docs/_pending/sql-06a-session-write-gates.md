## docs/INVARIANTS.md — RLS / DB conventions

Replace the bullet that starts "**Past-program sessions are read-only on the student surface.**" with the five bullets below. `docs/_pending/session-reviewed-lock.md` rewrites one sentence of that same bullet ("Enforced UI-side in `SessionView` via `isReadOnly` (derived from …)") and adds a bullet after it: the first bullet below already says what that sentence would say, so skip the first block of that snippet and add its bullet ("**A session the coach has reviewed is read-only for the athlete and its confirmation cannot be undone …**") after these five.

- **A session is read-only for its athlete when its program is not the block being trained, when the coach archived it, or when the coach reviewed it — and the database asks all three.** An athlete's write to anything that hangs under a session asks three questions: `student_profile_for_slot(x) = auth.uid()` (the row is mine), `program_active_for_slot(x) = true` (my program is the active, non-trashed one) and `session_writable_for_slot(x)` (the session is neither archived nor reviewed). Five surfaces carry them since migration `2026_10_12_08_session_write_gates.sql` (review P58): `set_logs`, `slot_comments`, `slot_deviations`, `set_log_videos` and the files of the `set-videos` bucket (`storage.objects`). Before it `set_logs` asked nothing about the session, `slot_comments` nothing about the review, `slot_deviations` asked "archived" on INSERT only, and videos — row and file — asked nothing at all, so a second device that still showed a reviewed session open kept rewriting what the coach had reviewed. Reading is never gated: SELECT is a policy of its own on each table, and an athlete sees every session they ever trained. Coach writes are never gated this way, and `adopt_swap`, `adopt_skip` and `decline_promote_request` write `slot_deviations` of a reviewed session as SECURITY DEFINER. `session_confirmations` is not one of the five: its policies ask "not archived" and "program active", not "not reviewed". UI-side the lock is `isReadOnly` in `SessionView`: `isClosed` (derived from `session.program_is_active` + `archived_at`) or `session.reviewed_at` set (see "A session the coach has reviewed is read-only for the athlete", below). `supabase/tests/session-gates.test.js` holds the database's half, with every write of the app replayed on a session in progress, confirmed, reviewed, archived, of a past block and of a trashed one.
- **`session_writable` is the lock of the SESSION and says nothing about the program.** `session_writable(session)` and `session_writable_for_slot(slot)` answer "not archived and not reviewed" — `true` for a session of a past block, of a trashed block or of a draft. Whether the program is the one being trained is `program_active_for_session` / `program_active_for_slot`, and a policy asks both. So does a SECURITY DEFINER function that writes for an athlete: it goes around the policies, and one that asked `session_writable` alone would write into past blocks. Both never answer NULL (a session that does not exist is not writable — `IF NOT session_writable(x) THEN RAISE` refuses what it should; keep the `COALESCE(…, false)`). What closes a session is said in that function, in one place: a new column that locks a session joins it, not the policies. `session_writable(uuid)` is not executable by a signed-in user — no policy calls it; `session_writable_for_slot` does, as its owner.
- **The lock is `reviewed_at`, not the confirmation.** Set-log writes replay in per-row scopes and the confirmation in `offline-writes`, so a set logged offline may arrive after the confirmation of its session. A confirmed session that the coach has not reviewed is written like one in progress. Do not move the gate to `session_confirmations`: the last sets of an offline workout would be refused. The two are independent in the other direction too: **a session can be reviewed and not confirmed** — a review page reached from a `session_deviation` notification while the athlete is still training, a confirmation undone after the review. Such a session is closed like any reviewed one, while `buildQueue` — which drops a session when it is confirmed or archived, never when it is reviewed — keeps listing it as to do. The way out for its athlete is the confirmation, which the database accepts on a reviewed session: `session_confirmations` is not one of the five surfaces. A client that shows such a session as open sends writes that are all refused; line 13 of the audit of migration 08 counts these sessions, and its runbook page ("Sessions reviewed that their athlete has not confirmed") lists them and gives the statement that takes a review back.
- **A refused athlete write has two shapes, and one of them is silent.** An INSERT — an upsert included, which PostgreSQL judges as the INSERT it starts as — raises `42501` (`errors.notAllowed`). An UPDATE or a DELETE on a closed session reaches no row and raises nothing: `.update(…).select().single()` then fails with PGRST116 (`errors.generic`), and a bare `.delete()` or `.update()` answers success with nothing changed. A hook that deletes or updates without reading the row back cannot tell "done" from "refused"; on a session that may be closed, gate the affordance in the UI or select the row.
- **A set video is stored at `<athlete's profile id>/<exercise slot of its set>/<file>`, and nowhere else.** The WITH CHECK of `set_log_videos` (INSERT and UPDATE) requires `storage_path` to be exactly there — `split_part(storage_path, '/', 1) = auth.uid()` and `set_video_slot(storage_path) = slot_for_set_log(set_log_id)` — and the storage policies judge a file by the slot its path names: an athlete adds or removes a file only under a slot of their own, in a writable session of their active program. So `useUploadSetVideo` must keep building the path from `user.id` and the set's own `exercise_slot_id`; a path under another slot is refused at the row (`42501`), and a file uploaded there cannot be attached to the set. Without the rule a row could point at a file under a session still to come, and that file stayed replaceable after the review. An account nobody coaches has no slot and uploads nothing (review L5); nobody uploads under another athlete's exercise. There is no UPDATE policy on the files: one is never overwritten or renamed. KNOWN residual gaps: a row written before the rule keeps whatever path it has (the audit of migration 08 counts them; the athlete can still read it, and remove it while its session is writable); a file whose row is gone stays in the bucket, readable by its athlete and their coach, and removable by the athlete only while the session of the slot in its path is writable.

Replace, in the bullet that starts "**Structural deviations: students can swap/skip an exercise", "RLS clones `slot_comments` (student owns the row, writes gated to the active non-archived program, coach reads)" with:

RLS clones `slot_comments` (student owns the row; INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE ask the three questions of every athlete write — own row, active program, session neither archived nor reviewed; coach reads)

Replace the bullet that starts "- **RLS gate** — student-side policies on `set_logs`, `session_confirmations`, and `slot_comments` are split" with:

- **RLS gate** — on `set_logs`, `slot_comments`, `slot_deviations` and `set_log_videos` the athlete has four policies: SELECT is permissive (own-row ownership only) so students can still read their own RPEs, done flags, notes and videos when they re-open a past session via the calendar; INSERT / UPDATE / DELETE ask three questions — `student_profile_for_slot(x) = auth.uid()`, `program_active_for_slot(x) = true`, `session_writable_for_slot(x)`. The helpers are SECURITY DEFINER and STABLE: `program_active_for_session(sess_id)` and `program_active_for_slot(slot_id)` walk session → week → program (migration `2026_04_26_lock_inactive_program_writes.sql`); `session_writable(sess_id)` and `session_writable_for_slot(slot_id)` read `archived_at` and `reviewed_at` of the session, and `slot_for_set_log(log_id)` gives the slot of a set for the policies of `set_log_videos` (migration `2026_10_12_08_session_write_gates.sql`). The two upload and delete policies on `storage.objects` ask the same three of the slot named by the path. `session_confirmations` keeps its own split policies (not archived, program active). Coach-side policies are unchanged so coaches can still edit past blocks and reviewed sessions.

## docs/ARCHITECTURE.md — Set video uploads

Replace the bullet that starts "- **Path convention**: `<profile_id>/<slot_id>/<set>-<uuid>.<ext>`." with:

- **Path convention**: `<profile_id>/<slot_id>/<set>-<uuid>.<ext>` — the athlete's profile id, then the exercise slot of the set. It is a rule of the database since migration `2026_10_12_08_session_write_gates.sql`: the athlete's INSERT and UPDATE of a `set_log_videos` row require `storage_path` to be under exactly those two folders, and the athlete's upload and delete policies on `storage.objects` require the first folder to be `auth.uid()` and the second (`public.set_video_slot(name)`) to be a slot of their own, in a session that is neither archived nor reviewed, of their active program. Reading is by first folder: `auth.uid()` for the athlete, a `profile_id` of one of their athletes for the coach.
