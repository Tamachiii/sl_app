The text below describes the tree with both lanes in it.

- **A confirmation, its undo and a note for the coach are on screen at the tap, in every read that shows them.** They are made at the end of a workout, in a gym: the write waits for a network more often than not, sometimes until the app has been closed and opened again. So `onMutate` writes everything the athlete is shown about it, and for a confirmation that is three reads, not one: `['session-confirmation', sessionId]` (the card on the session page), `['my-confirmed-session-ids', …]` (which session is "Next" on the training list, the place in the block, the "Done" drawer) and `['student-program-details', …]` (`sessions.performed_at` — the day that dates and orders the drawer and feeds the greeting; `performedAtOf` writes it the way `sync_session_performed_at` will, `<performed_on>T00:00:00+00:00`, or the moment of the confirmation when no day was sent, and `writeTrainingListSession` in [`hooks/useStudentProgramDetails.js`](../src/hooks/useStudentProgramDetails.js) finds the session in the list and in any past block that was opened). Written into the first alone, the session that had just been finished stayed the "Next" one of the list, with its Start button, until the confirmation reached the database (P25). `useUnconfirmSession` does the reverse on the same three; `useSaveSlotComment` writes the note — trimmed, as the database will hold it — into `['slot-comments', sessionId]`, or takes it out for an empty body. Whoever adds a read that shows "this session is done" adds it to these `onMutate`, and to `CONFIRMATION_READS` in `lib/offlineMutations.js` for the read that follows. Tests: `src/hooks/useSessionConfirmation.optimistic.test.jsx` and the second half of `src/hooks/useSlotComments.test.jsx` (requests that stay open until the test answers them), `src/components/student/SessionView.confirm.test.jsx` (the session page and the training list on the real hooks, without a network, across a relaunch and when the network returns).
- **A list the device does not hold is not made by a guess, and the one row it knows is.** `['my-confirmed-session-ids']` and the block are written only where an entry already holds data: a list of confirmed sessions made of the one just confirmed would put every other session of the block back to "not done". `['session-confirmation', sessionId]` is made, as before — it is one row, and a session read ahead by the training list has never had its confirmation read. So are the notes of a session whose notes were never read, with the one note the athlete has just written, and that entry is flagged at once (`invalidateQueries({ exact: true, refetchType: 'none' })`) so that it is read as soon as it can be. The two lists therefore have to be in memory still when the athlete confirms, an hour after leaving the page that reads them — nothing on the session page observes them. That is the `gcTime` of the persisted roots (`PERSIST_MAX_AGE_MS` in [`lib/queryClient.js`](../src/lib/queryClient.js), held by `src/lib/queryClient.cache.test.js`): with React Query's five minutes they are collected during the workout, the confirmation is written into nothing, and the list the athlete comes back to has the session as "Next" again. The tests of the confirmation build their clients with `gcTime: Infinity` and would not see that default go.
- **A write that failed takes back what it put on screen — unless a newer write to the same thing is pending.** Confirm, undo, confirm again without a network is three writes, and what the athlete sees is the state after the last of them: `hasNewerWrite` in [`hooks/useSessionConfirmation.js`](../src/hooks/useSessionConfirmation.js) (any other pending write of the two confirmation keys on that session) and `hasNewerSave` in [`hooks/useSlotComments.js`](../src/hooks/useSlotComments.js) (same exercise) make the `onError` of an earlier one stand down. These writes leave one at a time, in the order they were made (the `offline-writes` scope), so another pending one is always a later one. Otherwise `takeBack` restores what the context holds, which is a few values and not the lists — the confirmation as it was, `list: 'added' | 'removed' | null` (so a session that was already in the list is not taken out of it), and the day with what it replaced, put back only where the session still holds what this write put there. Two traps. `setQueryData(key, undefined)` writes nothing: a confirmation that had never been read is restored as `previous ?? null`, or the card stays on "Session confirmed" after a confirmation the database refused. And none of this runs for a write restored after a relaunch — it has the defaults of its key and no hook: what puts the screen right then is the read that follows it (`refreshAfter`), except while a replay is being refused: a confirmation the database turns down at that moment stays on the card and in the list until their reads go stale.
- **A guess is written through `writeCached`, never with `setQueryData` alone.** `setQueryData` stamps the entry as read just now and takes its "to be read again" flag off. For an optimistic update that is one statement too many: the training list an offline confirmation was written into would count as fresh for two more minutes — not read again when the athlete comes back to it on a network, after a workout during which the coach may have rewritten the block — and an entry invalidated without a network would forget that it is owed a read. `writeCached(queryClient, queryKey, update)` in [`lib/cacheWrite.js`](../src/lib/cacheWrite.js) writes every entry the key matches with the `dataUpdatedAt` it had and puts the flag back, skips an entry that holds nothing, and leaves an entry alone when `update` answers `undefined` or the same data. `['session-confirmation', sessionId]` is the exception that stays on `setQueryData` — it has to be made — and the defaults read it again when the write settles. Tests: `src/lib/cacheWrite.test.js`.
- **"Waits for a network" is asked of the queue, and `isPending` is not it.** A write parked offline is `pending` for as long as the device has no network — hours. Three things follow in [`SessionView.jsx`](../src/components/student/SessionView.jsx). (1) A button is held by a write that is BEING SENT, `isPending && !isPaused` (`confirmInFlight`, `undoInFlight`): keyed on `isPending`, "Confirm session" stayed switched off and on "Confirming…" for a session confirmed and then un-confirmed in the gym, and "Undo confirmation" after an undo followed by a new confirm. (2) The line "Saved on this device. It will be sent as soon as you're back online." (`sessionConfirm.waiting`, `role="status"`, inside the confirmed card) comes from `useConfirmationWaiting(sessionId)`: a `useIsMutating` over the confirm key, paused, for that session — read from the mutation cache and not from `useConfirmSession()`, because the confirmation is usually still waiting after the page was left and after a relaunch, where it is a mutation restored from IndexedDB that no hook of this launch has seen. (3) "Paused" alone is not "offline": React Query also calls a write paused while it stands behind another one of its scope — a note being saved — for a few hundred milliseconds on a working network. So the hook also asks `useOnlineStatus()` and `onlineManager.isOnline()`, the second because it is the one that parks a write.
- **The confirm dialog closes at the tap, and the field of a note when it is left — neither waits for an answer.** The page behind them shows the result from that moment, and the answer can be hours away: a parked write is neither a success nor a failure, and both used to sit open on it (the dialog on "Confirming…" with Cancel switched off, `onClose` ignored). `handleConfirm` calls `mutate` and closes; the buttons of the dialog are never disabled. What was typed is kept for the case the write fails. The dialog's note leaves its field in the `onSuccess` of the call, so after a refusal — the hook has put "Confirm session" back — the dialog opens on it. That answer takes out of the field what it carried and nothing else (`current === written`): it can come hours after the tap, to an athlete who has taken the confirmation back, opened the dialog again and is writing the note of the next one. [`SlotCommentBox.jsx`](../src/components/student/SlotCommentBox.jsx) keeps what a failed save carried (`unsaved`, set by the `onError` of the call) and opens on it next time; it does not reopen by itself, which on a phone is a keyboard coming up in the middle of a set. The box fills its draft when it OPENS (`openEditor`), not by an effect on the note: the note changes under an open field for other reasons than typing — a save that is taken back, a read that comes in — and the effect replaced what was being typed.
- **The confirm dialog says what is confirmed, and asks twice when nothing is logged.** It opens with "Sets logged: N of M" (`sessionConfirm.summary`): the sets the athlete has dealt with — done, failed, skipped, or under an exercise they skipped — which is what the progress bar counts (`resolvedCount`), so a session at 100% is not confirmed as "10 of 12". With M > 0 and N = 0 the first tap on Confirm sends nothing: it shows `sessionConfirm.nothingLogged` (`role="alert"`) and the button becomes "Confirm anyway"; closing the dialog forgets that it asked. A session can be confirmed without a set — trained and not ticked, or not trained and said so in the note — but not by a slip of the thumb: the coach is notified and the session leaves "Next" (live item N8). A session with no set at all has nothing to count and is confirmed in one tap.
- **A test that leaves a write parked empties its client before the network is put back.** `onlineManager` is one object for a whole test file, and the page or the hook a test rendered is still mounted in `afterEach`: `onlineManager.setOnline(true)` there sends whatever the test left waiting. That request gets no answer, or one nobody reads; and with the retry policy of the queued writes (`mutationRetry`, `mutationRetryDelay`) a write that got no answer is sent again one, two and four seconds later — inside another test, into whatever stands for the database there. `SessionView.confirm.test.jsx` failed that way in half of its runs or more: the confirmation its first test leaves in the gym took the refusal a later test had set for its own. The three test files of the confirmation and of the notes that take the network away (`SessionView.confirm.test.jsx`, `useSessionConfirmation.optimistic.test.jsx`, `useSlotComments.test.jsx`) keep the clients they make and call `clear()` on each in `afterEach`, before `setOnline(true)`.
